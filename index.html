Why choose Medex?

For over 70 years we have turned the wisdom of the hive into products that families trust. Four things guide everything we make.

Our story
  1. 01

    Bees as inspiration

    For over 70 years, bees have been our role model. Their diligence, cooperation and respect for nature guide everything we make, as they have since 1954.

  2. 02

    Innovation

    We combine the wisdom of the hive with modern science. Together with experts we develop standardised formulas, such as royal jelly standardised for 10-HDA and propolis in alcohol- and water-based forms.

  3. 03

    Quality

    Every batch is checked in the laboratory, from raw material to finished product. The honey we fill is tested, safe and of controlled quality, and you can check it yourself.

  4. 04

    Sustainability

    We work with beekeepers who look after healthy colonies, and we care for the meadows and forests that bees, and all of us, depend on.
